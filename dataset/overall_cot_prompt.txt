Step 2: 2D localization.
For each part, the visible image position is recorded by `bbox_2d`.
Part `l_<id>`: `bbox_2d` = [cx, cy, w, h].
...
(Normalized to [0.000, 1.000]; origin at top-left; format: center_x, center_y, width, height.)

Step 3: Topology and adjacency.
For each part, local spatial connectivity is recorded by `spatial_relation`.
Part `l_<id>`: `adjacent_parts` = [`l_<id>`, ...].
...
(List all parts that are physically contacting or directly connected in 3D space.)

Step 4: Primitive abstraction.
For each part, the coarse 3D primitive abstraction is recorded by `primitive_shape`.
Part `l_<id>`: `shape_label` = cuboid | cylinder | sphere.
...

Step 5: Surface perception.
For each part, the surface perceptual state is recorded by `surface_features`.
Part `l_<id>`: `hardness` = soft | semi_rigid | rigid, `roughness` = smooth | textured | rough, `reflectivity` = matte | glossy | highly_reflective, `transparency` = opaque | translucent | transparent.
...
</think>
